Vibe Tank is quizbowl practice that feels like a 2008 desktop came to life. Your host, Neptuna Glossberry, a sentient aquarium screensaver, reads each question one word at a time. Buzz the moment you know it. Get it before the power mark for 15 points, but a wrong guess while she's still reading costs you 5.

Real quizbowl, built for practice

Pyramidal tossups and three-part bonuses across History, Literature, Science, Fine Arts, Religion, Mythology, Philosophy, Social Science, Geography, Current Events and more
Five difficulty levels: Middle School, Easy High School, Regular High School, Hard High School and National High School
Packets built like a real tournament distribution, or pick exactly the categories you want
Protest a ruling and get a fair second look

Study like it's Anki

Missed questions automatically come back tomorrow, then at longer and longer intervals as you learn them
Browse every question to star, queue, suspend or drill specific sets
Track your points per 20 tossups heard (PP20TUH), powers, negs and accuracy by category

Play your way

Choose a companion that changes the rules: the Fox scores 25 for early buzzes, the Penguin never negs, the Cheetah reads faster for bigger powers
Add modifiers like Sudden Death or Double or Nothing when you want chaos
Race friends in live rooms, where the earliest correct buzz wins

A world to study in
Pick from 14 biomes (forest, jungle, desert, tundra, coral reef and more), each with glossy animals, or go clean with Study Paper or Black & White. Customize fonts, windows, buttons, your host's name and color, and play along to a shuffled Frutiger Aero soundtrack.
